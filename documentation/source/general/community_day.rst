Eclipse Qrisp Community Day
============================

`Click here to register <https://luma.com/56k6pve5>`__ and be part of the very first Eclipse Qrisp Community Day!

Join the Qrisp Community in Berlin for a full day dedicated to quantum programming with Qrisp. The event will feature technical talks, community discussions, hands-on insights, and opportunities to connect with the people building and using Qrisp.

.. list-table::
    :widths: 20 80

    * - 💶 **Participation**
      - Free of charge!
    * - 🗓️ **Date**
      - October 29th, 2026
    * - 📍 **Location**
      - Fraunhofer FOKUS, Kaiserin-Augusta-Allee 31, 10589 Berlin
    * - 🤝 **Organized by**
      - Eclipse Foundation, in scientific cooperation with Fraunhofer FOKUS

Social Dinner
-------------

Join us the evening before, on October 28th, for a casual get-together at Arminius Markthalle Berlin (self-paid basis) — a great chance to connect before the main event.
